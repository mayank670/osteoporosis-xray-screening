Bone health · AI research prototype

A gentle first look at bone health from a knee X-ray

OsteoScreen shows how a deep-learning model reads knee X-rays, and explains what it looked at.

  1. 1Upload a knee X-ray. PNG, JPEG, TIFF or BMP.
  2. 2Add a few details such as age and weight. They make the result far more reliable.
  3. 3Read the result in plain words, with a heatmap and a PDF you can keep.

{{ icon('shield') }} Your scans are private to your account. OsteoScreen is a student research project, not a diagnosis. Bone density is confirmed with a DXA scan by a doctor.
